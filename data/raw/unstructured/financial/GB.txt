Green Bay Packers — 2026 franchise valuation: $8.59 billion (Sportico), up 33% year-over-year, ranked #22 among all 32 NFL teams. Dropped 4 positions, the largest ranking decline in the NFC this cycle. The Packers are the NFL's only publicly-held, non-profit franchise structure and are effectively unable to be sold in the way other franchises are — a unique ownership structure that limits the same kind of sale-driven valuation spikes seen elsewhere (e.g. Seattle) but also insulates the team from single-owner financial instability.
