Dallas Cowboys — 2026 franchise valuation: $15.5 billion (Sportico), up 21% year-over-year, ranked #1 among all 32 NFL teams. First franchise to surpass the $15 billion mark. Remains the league's financial standard despite an on-field playoff drought dating back to 1995, underscoring that valuation growth is driven primarily by media rights, market size, and business operations rather than recent postseason success.
